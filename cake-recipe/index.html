Cinnamon-Lemon Warm Drink

A simple warm drink made with cinnamon, lemon, and a pinch of salt. These are the ingredients and steps shown in the clip.

01 / Ingredients

What you’ll need

  • Water for one serving (the video doesn’t specify an amount)
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • A pinch of Himalayan salt
  • The juice of ½ lemon

02 / Method

How to make it

  1. Bring water to a boil in a small pot.
  2. Add the cinnamon and a pinch of salt. Squeeze in the juice of half a lemon.
  3. Boil for 10 minutes.
  4. Strain into a mug and let it cool to a comfortable sipping temperature.

Note: The video doesn’t give a water measurement. Use enough for one serving and adjust the amount to taste.
