Sweet Potato Blueberry Cake

A simple cake made with baked sweet potatoes, eggs, blueberries, and baking powder. The clip’s caption says to bake the potatoes rather than boil them.

01 / Ingredients

What you’ll need

  • 2 sweet potatoes, baked, cooled, peeled, and mashed
  • 3 eggs
  • A handful of frozen blueberries
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

02 / Method

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking tin.
  2. Bake the sweet potatoes until soft; don’t boil them. Let them cool, then peel and mash.
  3. Mix the mashed sweet potato with the eggs and baking powder.
  4. Fold in the frozen blueberries and spoon the mixture into the lined tin.
  5. Bake for 30 minutes.
