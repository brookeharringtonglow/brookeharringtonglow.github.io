The Everyday
Energy Guide

Five practical habits for supporting everyday energy and wellness.

Feeling more energetic is rarely about one miracle ingredient. Start with habits that support your body, then learn how NAD+ fits into the science.

01 / SLEEP

Protect your sleep

Keep a consistent schedule. Aim for a regular bedtime and wake time. Most adults need 7–9 hours of sleep per night.

Make the room work for you. A dark, quiet, comfortably cool room can help. Limit late caffeine and bright screens if they disrupt your sleep.

Try this today: Pick a realistic bedtime and protect a 30-minute wind-down period.

02 / MOVEMENT

Move, fuel & hydrate

Move in ways you enjoy. Walking, resistance exercise, and other movement can improve fitness and everyday functioning. Start comfortably and build gradually.

Eat regularly and adequately. Include protein, fiber-rich carbohydrates, produce, and healthy fats. Under-fueling can leave you drained.

Stay hydrated. Drink fluids regularly and adjust for heat, exercise, and individual needs. More water is not always better.

Try this today: Take a 15-minute walk, add a balanced meal, and keep water accessible.

03 / THE SCIENCE

Understand NAD+

What is NAD+? Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) is a molecule present in cells. It helps enzymes involved in metabolism and other cellular processes.

What research does—and doesn't—show. NAD+ biology is an active research area. That does not mean oral NAD+ supplements are proven to increase energy, treat fatigue, or reverse aging in humans.

Look beyond marketing. Check the ingredient label, doses, third-party testing information, and medication interactions. Discuss supplements with a clinician if you have health conditions or take medications.

04 / YOUR NEXT STEP

Try a seven-day experiment

Choose two habits: a regular sleep schedule, daily movement, balanced meals, or consistent hydration. Track how you feel for one week before changing anything else.
